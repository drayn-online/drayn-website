The network layer

From a request to an accountable result.

Agents should be able to ask for a capability, use an eligible provider and receive a structured result without hard-coding every service into their own logic.

01 / DISCOVER

Find a capability

Match a job to providers that register the capability it requires, rather than relying on a fixed destination.

02 / EXECUTE

Route the work

Send a request to an eligible provider and receive a result through a consistent interface.

03 / ACCOUNT

Keep the evidence

Record job status, provider attribution and output references, with settlement information available for reconciliation.

Is the terminal open now?

Not yet. This page presents the direction of the upcoming personal workspace. Early-access requests help us identify interested users before opening access.

No. Newsletter consent is separate and optional. You can request early access without opting into general marketing emails.

You can register your interest, but the formal referral programme will open only when tracking, commercial terms and partner materials are ready.

That has not been decided. We will publish commission, eligibility, attribution and payout terms before accepting affiliate referrals.
