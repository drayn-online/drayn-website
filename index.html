What survives a move?

Eric is the DRAYN agent in our first continuity experiment. We compare its replies before and after a move from ElizaOS to OpenClaw. Can it recognise when a newer decision supersedes an older one? Can it apply an outstanding commitment and respect a disclosure boundary?

A fresh-agent control tests whether the same probes can be answered without imported history. A separate set of independently written probes is reserved for validation. We invite teams to apply the method to their own agents once the protocol is published.
