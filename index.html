01 — The idea

Not another chatbot.
Not just another token.

DRAYN is an experiment in persistent intelligence. The goal is to explore what happens when an intelligence can build upon its own history — observing, reasoning, remembering and eventually acting with continuity over time.

This is not a finished product. It is the beginning of a process, and that process is being built in public.

02 — Principles

Four things to learn.

01

Observe.

Understand the information, environment and context presented to it.

02

Reason.

Move beyond retrieval toward structured thought, comparison and decision-making.

03

Remember.

Build memory that persists and meaningfully influences what comes next.

04

Act.

Eventually connect intelligence and memory to useful actions in the world.

Memory is not simply stored information.
Memory changes what comes next.
