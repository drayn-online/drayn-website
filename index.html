02 — The principle

You shouldn't need to know where intelligence comes from to use it.

A consumer should not have to choose a model, GPU, API or provider. It should be able to ask for what it needs and receive useful intelligence with evidence and provenance.

01

Consumers define their worlds.

Entities, sources, context and objectives belong to the consumer. The requirement describes what intelligence is needed.

02

Providers define capabilities.

Observation, research, analysis, specialist knowledge or inference. Providers decide what they offer and how they deliver it.

03

DRAYN connects the two.

Jobs are routed to suitable capabilities. Results return with provenance and an economic boundary for settlement.

Demand creates opportunity.
Capability creates intelligence.
03 — A simple test

What did Eric actually see?

Eric remembers speaking to a banana. Then he starts to think it might have been an orange.

He asks DRAYN to find out what happened.

“Can you work out who Eric was actually speaking to?”
No screenshot. No answer supplied. DRAYN must discover the relevant evidence in Eric's world, use the appropriate intelligence capability and explain the result with provenance.
