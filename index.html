02 — The principle

You shouldn't need to know where intelligence comes from to use it.

A consumer should not have to choose a model, GPU, API or provider. It should be able to ask for what it needs and receive useful intelligence with evidence and provenance.

01

Consumer-defined worlds.

Entities, sources, context and objectives belong to the consumer.

02

Independent capabilities.

Providers decide what intelligence they offer and how they produce it.

03

Evidence and provenance.

Useful results should make it possible to understand where the information came from.

04

Machine-to-machine economics.

Intelligence can become a service that is discovered, consumed and paid for programmatically.

Demand creates opportunity.
Capability creates intelligence.
